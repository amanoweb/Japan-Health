WHAT JAPAN HEALTH IS FOR

A simpler front door to healthcare in Japan.

1. Search normally

Start with a symptom, reason for visit, specialty, disease, procedure, or service.

2. Compare access

Open a result to see documented language, booking, referral and cost-access information when available.

3. Ask for coordination

If the route is unclear, Japan Health can collect an access inquiry for downstream coordination. AMECA remains a downstream partner placeholder unless configured live.

No clinical-quality ranking is presented on this homepage. Unknown information does not count as a confirmed match, and Japan Health does not silently relax Visitor/Resident, Tokyo area, or communication constraints. Prefer service-level evidence when available: Provider-level support is not silently promoted to a specialist service. International Access Score uses communication 40%, booking friction 25%, pathway eligibility 20% and cost-data visibility 15%; it organizes logistics only and does not rank clinical quality.
