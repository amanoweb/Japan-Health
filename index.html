MAJOR MEDICAL TRAVEL

Why a medical coordinator may be necessary.

Some large hospitals and international-patient pathways require or strongly rely on advance case review, medical-record transfer, translation, cost estimates or a facilitator before an overseas patient can book. Japan Health should explain that friction first, then compare coordination companies on documented capabilities.

No coordinator company is ranked here yet. “Best for cancer”, “best English”, pricing, hospital relationships and acceptance success rates should only be shown after each claim is verified from the company or hospital source.
