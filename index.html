WHAT JAPAN HEALTH IS FOR

A simpler front door to healthcare in Japan.

1. Start with normal language

Search by a symptom, reason for visit, specialty, disease, procedure, or service. You should not need to know Japanese medical terminology before you can start looking.

2. Respect your access constraints

Visitor vs resident, Tokyo area, and communication requirements are treated as real constraints. Japan Health does not silently relax them when producing results.

3. Show what is actually documented

Prefer service-level evidence when available. Provider-level support is not silently promoted to a specialist service, and unknown information remains visibly unknown.

No clinical-quality ranking is being presented on this homepage. Ranking methodology will be reviewed separately. Unknown does not count as a confirmed match. International Access Score uses communication 40%, booking friction 25%, pathway eligibility 20% and cost-data visibility 15%; it organizes documented logistics only and does not rank clinical quality.
