How evidence matching and the International Access Score work

When you search a disease or procedure, Japan Health separately checks whether the provider record contains matching expertise evidence and whether the record documents direct physician English or an interpreter pathway. A source-backed expertise match is not proof of clinical superiority or individual suitability, and a language pathway must still be confirmed for the requested specialist service.

The International Access Score separately combines documented communication access (40%), booking friction (25%), pathway eligibility fit (20%) and cost-data visibility (15%). Missing or unknown fields reduce confidence. It is a logistics-friction score only — never a measure of clinical quality, outcomes, popularity or physician skill.

Total-cost readiness is shown separately as medical, interpreter and coordinator cost components recorded vs needing confirmation. It measures data completeness only and is not a quote, affordability score or guaranteed total.

Tokyo baseline records only claim what the cited source supports. Physician-level English, reception English, interpretation, insurance, self-pay status, referrals, current availability and exact prices remain “unknown” unless a more specific official source supports them. A source-backed disease or procedure page does not guarantee that the same language pathway applies to that service.
