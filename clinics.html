How the International Access Score works

The score combines documented communication access (40%), booking friction (25%), pathway eligibility fit (20%) and cost-data visibility (15%). Missing or unknown fields reduce confidence. It is a logistics-friction score only — never a measure of clinical quality, outcomes, popularity or physician skill.

Tokyo baseline records only claim what the cited source supports. Physician-level English, reception English, interpretation, insurance, self-pay status, referrals, current availability and exact prices remain “unknown” unless a more specific official source supports them.
