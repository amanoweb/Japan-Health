What the International Access Score means

The International Access Score combines documented communication access (40%), booking friction (25%), pathway eligibility fit (20%) and cost-data visibility (15%). Missing or unknown fields reduce confidence. It is a logistics-friction score only — never a measure of clinical quality, outcomes, popularity or physician skill.

Disease/procedure evidence, requested-service language evidence, total-cost readiness and booking readiness are shown separately where the current record supports them. They describe documentation and access logistics, not clinical superiority or guaranteed acceptance.

Provider claims are limited to what the current record and cited sources support. Physician English, interpretation, eligibility, referral rules, current availability and exact prices remain unknown unless specifically documented. Demo records are illustrative and unverified.
