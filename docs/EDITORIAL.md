# Japan Health: editorial and advertising policy

## Source hierarchy
1. Japanese official law, policy, insurer or regulator for Japanese legal and insurance claims.
2. Official medical society guidelines / peer-reviewed evidence for clinical claims.
3. Provider or agency website for **attributed claims about that institution only**.
4. Public discussion forums as topic-discovery input only; never as proof of clinical quality.

Do not turn a general study finding into a claim about availability, pricing, insurance eligibility, or appointment policy at an individual Japanese institution.

## Claim record
Each material statement should have a source URL, a relevant excerpt or document location, jurisdiction, covered population, published date (if known), effective-from and effective-to (if known), retrieval date, editor and review status.

Unknown dates and requirements stay unknown; they must not be filled in by an LLM. Statements may be complementary, contradictory, narrower in scope, or unsupported. Semantic similarity alone is never evidence of agreement.

## Temporal publication gate
For an article dated D, check whether sources are the latest found through the defined retrieval procedure as of D; distinguish release date, legal effective date and fetch date. A new notice that starts in the future does not replace a currently effective rule. A successful scheduled job proves only that monitored sources were checked, not that all possible updates were found.

Do not automatically publish content based on an LLM's claim that it has verified a source. High-stakes medical, legal, financial, visa and hospital-access claims require human verification. If uncertain, revise, qualify or withhold.

## Advertising
Clearly label any paid sponsored section, paid profile or paid article. Payment can change labeled placement; it does not change objective listing evidence or medical judgments. Medical promotion requires review under applicable Japanese medical-advertising law. Do not scrape or republish third-party reviews wholesale.

## Privacy and inquiries
No medical-intake form or automatic third-party data transmission exists in this version. Future lead-sharing would require approved agreements, appropriate consent and specific legal review, including possible referral-fee and cross-border data issues.

## Updates
When any source changes, open a review item identifying affected claims, articles and language variants. Never silently change the date of a human medical review. Translations require independent review of terminology and applicability.
